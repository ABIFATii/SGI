Our Story
WHO
WE ARE

Strategy Governance Initiative (SGI) was born out of a deep conviction that the future of Nigeria's governance lies with a disciplined, informed, and purposeful generation of leaders.

What began as a student-led movement within campus politics has evolved into a structured national initiative — bridging the gap between campus activism and real-world governance. Our journey has taken us from Faculty Unions and Student Government bodies to active engagement with NGOs, policy circles, and national advocacy platforms.

Today, SGI stands as a premier governance initiative — not a partisan organisation, but a civic institution dedicated to building the human capital that democratic Nigeria demands.

Our Mission

To connect, train, and deploy a new generation of civic leaders committed to transparent, equitable, and people-centred governance across Nigeria.

Our Vision

A Nigeria where every community has access to disciplined, accountable governance — driven by youth who are structurally prepared to lead.

Growth Timeline
01
2020 — FOUNDATION
Campus Roots

Founded within student union politics, SGI emerged as a platform for structured, governance-focused student leadership across university campuses.

02
2022 — EXPANSION
Multi-Campus Presence

Extended reach to universities across multiple states. Key members participated in the NAPPAD Constitutional Review Committee, signalling entry into institutional policy work.

03
2023 — STRUCTURE
National Working Committee

Formalised the National Working Committee (NWC), establishing clear leadership hierarchy, zonal coordinators, and a national headquarters framework.

04
2024 — IMPACT
NGO & Policy Engagement

Transitioned from student activism to national civic engagement — partnering with NGOs, contributing to policy discourse, and building a 500+ member network.

05
2025 — TODAY
Governance Initiative

SGI is now a nationally recognised governance initiative — bridging grassroots communities with national leadership structures across 18 states.
